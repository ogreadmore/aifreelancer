# Florida Tattoo: case brief

Prepared November 16, 2026 by AI Freelancer. Version 1.0.

## Who this is for

Brissa Behrens, also known professionally as Brissa Renee, owns Subculturez Tattoo & Piercing in Orange City, Florida, and Anatomy Divine in Altamonte Springs, Florida. The reports address her communication and customer relationships during a social-media controversy.

## What brought the situation here

Temperance, who posts as @temperance777, described harassment at Kia Center while using a wheelchair. The public story connected Taylor, a Subculturez manager, to the incident. A November 13 news report described the shop’s apology and announcement that Taylor was no longer employed or affiliated with the business. The original social-media announcement was not accessible through the logged-out route examined during this review. [News report: S01](evidence.html#S01) · [Announcement: S05](evidence.html#S05).

The discussion also turned to Brissa’s own dismissive comments. A circulated screenshot contains the phrases “little girl drama” and “That has nothing to do with me.” The commissioning client confirmed that screenshot is authentic. The coaching report addresses those words directly. [Comment and provenance: S04](evidence.html#S04).

By the November 16 review, Temperance’s newer public posts asked people to stop review attacks and harassment, including at unrelated shops. One explicitly acknowledged that the manager had already been fired. That is a material development beyond the earlier article. It does not establish private reconciliation. [Clarification: S02](evidence.html#S02) · [Newer response: S03](evidence.html#S03).

## What the reports are trying to accomplish

Make the owner’s response accessible; acknowledge her dismissive wording; reduce unproductive arguments; answer customer concerns; and support respectful private contact only if appropriate. Report 01 is a plan Brissa can carry out herself. Report 02 ranks the functions an exceptional, fully resourced team would provide.

The work is communications advice. The commission accepts the business’s reported actions and fixes and does not reopen the underlying incident investigation. Staff safety and operational management are outside this assignment. We do not infer employment at Anatomy Divine from shared ownership.

## What remains open

- Whether private contact or resolution has already occurred, and whether further contact is welcome.
- The complete spoken content of the eight-minute response video; the review covered visible captions and selected comments, not a full audio transcript.
- Whether the social-profile access limitations observed in a logged-out browser are temporary or viewer-specific.
- Actual booking, cancellation, revenue, and customer-retention outcomes. No reliable baseline was supplied.

These gaps affect particular recommendations. They are not a request to delay the basic communication work or to question the confirmed screenshot.
