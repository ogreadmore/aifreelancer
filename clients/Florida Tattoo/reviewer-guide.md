Third-party media remains at the original links; the package contains selected excerpts and observation notes, not full copies of articles or videos.

## What a useful review should do

- Identify a concrete problem, point to the exact report section, explain why it matters, and provide replacement wording or a specific action.
- Prioritize the three changes most likely to improve clarity, credibility, or customer outcomes.
- Check that a reader with no previous context understands who Brissa, Taylor, and Temperance are, and why two shops appear in the reports.
- Check that advice is realistic for an owner doing the work herself, and that the unlimited-budget report adds meaningful professional execution.
- Test navigation, mobile reading, draft-copy controls, source links, and print layout where possible.
- Distinguish facts, observed source text, professional judgment, and proposed actions. Suggested statements are drafts, not evidence of something already published.
- Flag new material evidence with its original URL and review date. Preserve older observations as dated records rather than silently overwriting them.

## Suggested review format

For each finding: priority; file and section; current wording; problem; proposed replacement; evidence or rationale; whether any new information is needed. Finish with any important gaps that cannot be resolved from the package.

Changes to the website should be reviewed in this conversation before incorporation. This guide is a request for critique; it does not grant a reviewer authority to contact anyone involved, publish statements on the shops’ behalf, or modify external accounts.
