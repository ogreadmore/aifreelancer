Filter rules and source independence
  • Read this as a dependency-aware evidence stack, not a weighted score, surname probability or series of automatic passes. Cells qualify the exact proposition and reviewed scope.
  • The same source or test reused across layers is not independent corroboration. Adding status cells cannot create a probability. Shared A823 supports broad branch compatibility only, not surname-era closeness; a missing autosomal attribution does not block investigation of the Y line.
  • support means the stated bounded observation is supported, not that a candidate family passes every filter. limited means a real source-role/identity/coverage limit. unresolved means a performed review leaves the question open. not_tested means the specified test was not executed or lacked an identified target; it is neither absence nor incompatibility.
  • FS01 and FS02 preserve the existing genetic and documentary screens. All 24 FL classifications, nine HX assessments, display groups and ranks remain unchanged.
  • A chronology contradiction applies only to the exact identity proposal. The younger 1866 namesake is not the elder Aaron who died by December 1826; his surname and possible family relationship are not excluded.
  • Keep official execution, retrospectively reported officiation, earlier parcel interest, personal association and explicit kinship distinct. Variants are discovery forms, not identity proof.
  • FS06 requires two separately documented bridges: elder Aaron to a candidate historical paternal family, and that historical person's independently documented father–son chain to the exact comparator. One cannot repair the other.
  • A recorded heir, uncle or other kinship term does not itself demonstrate biological male-line transmission. Verify each father–son route separately and retain possible maternal, adoptive or marital relationships until the sources distinguish them.
  • FS07 uses time-matched, independently documented paternal localities and migration/kinship evidence. Country labels, surname rarity, STR distance and ancient branch models do not date or orient a surname transition.
  • FS08 is parallel autosomal corroboration, not a mandatory Y-line gate. One joined/nested segment group is not multiple independent ancestral confirmations; lack of an inherited segment does not exclude a lineage.
  • Dependencies identify missing inputs for interpretation, not permission to repeat saved searches, pending requests or unchanged access gates. Apply precise next tests only when a new pointer or the named data/source dependency changes.
  • Dependencies are reusable evidence inputs, not requirements that earlier filters must first pass. A parish, migration or autosomal source may help supply an earlier identity/kinship bridge; each lane can be investigated when its own specific inputs permit.
